# Tier 2 — Team Transformation Gameplan

**The move:** add one creative-focused hire who owns SMRT Social; Tony keeps Ads,
Jason keeps Sites. Build a real marketing learning center, push daily questions
to main SMRT support, and convert the team's freed time into (a) growing the
three owned services and (b) a productized **Managed Marketing** offer.

The economic logic: Tier 1 makes the software self-serve and cheap; Tier 2 makes
the *expertise* the paid product. Same team, opposite revenue direction — from
answering questions for free to selling outcomes.

---

|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tony** | SMRT Ads | $1.1K/mo · 3 clients | **$7.5K/mo · 15 clients** | Managed Marketing lead, paid sessions, pricing/billing handoff to finance |

Time split guidance (so growth targets are physically possible):
- New hire: **50% Social delivery / 30% shared creative / 20% content for learning center**
- Tony: 40% Ads / 30% Managed Marketing & sessions / 30% external marketing (SMRT's own)
- Jason: 40% Sites / 30% sessions & managed / 30% Tier 3 product input + learning center

## 2. Self-service engine (the learning center)

Goal: a customer can go from zero → first campaign sent without talking to anyone.

| Component | Content | Owner | Done by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Getting-started track | 5 guides + 5 short videos: connect Twilio number, first broadcast, first trigger campaign, reviews setup, reading the dashboard | Jason (writing) + hire (video/visuals) | Day 45 |
| Playbook library | 12 recipe playbooks: winback, birthday, first-visit follow-up, referral, seasonal (comforter/wedding/holiday), review velocity, lapsed VIP, route-day reminder | Tony | Day 60 |
| Module how-tos | Per-screen articles for Campaigns, Broadcasts, Reviews, promos, segments | Migrate/refresh existing Notion support pages | Day 60 |
| Compliance & deliverability | TCPA basics, quiet hours, frequency caps, segment costs ($0.014), opt-out handling | Tony | Day 45 — **launch gate** |
| In-app assists | Tooltips + "?" links from each marketing screen into the learning center; pre-seeded templates so new attaches start loaded | with dev team | Day 90 |
| Support enablement | Ticket taxonomy, 20 macros, escalation matrix, 2-week shadowing rotation | Tony + support lead | Day 30–45 |

Deflection loop: every escalated ticket that support can't answer becomes next
week's article/macro. Review the top-10 ticket drivers monthly; the learning
center is finished when the top drivers are all self-serve.

## 3. Managed Marketing (the paid expertise ladder)

The freed hours get sold back to the customers who *don't* want self-service —
this is also the retention play for accounts hit by per-station repricing.

| Offer | Price | What they get | Delivery cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Success session | $100/hr | Working session with Tony or Jason | 1 hr |
| Campaign setup (one-time) | $499 | Full trigger-campaign suite configured + 3 branded templates | ~4 hrs |
| Creative pack | $199 | 3 custom-branded templates (hire) | ~2 hrs |
| **Managed Campaigns** | $299/mo | Monthly calendar: 2 campaigns + 1 broadcast, run for them, monthly report | ~2.5 hrs/mo |
| **Managed Growth** | $599/mo | Everything managed: campaigns + reviews + quarterly strategy call | ~5 hrs/mo |
| SMRT Social (existing) | $299/mo | Already a managed service — the hire's book of business | ~3 hrs/mo |

Capacity check (the honest ceiling): Tony + Jason at ~30% managed/sessions time
≈ 100 hrs/mo combined → **~25–30 managed accounts or equivalent mix ≈
$8–12K/mo**, before hiring delivery help. Managed demand beyond ~30 accounts is
the trigger to add a second (delivery, non-creative) hire — a good problem.

## 5. 30/60/90/180 gameplan

| Window | Moves |
|---|---|
| **Days 1–30** | Post the creative/Social role. Ticket taxonomy + macro list with support. Freeze: no new ad-hoc free work — everything becomes a session, a package, or an article. Move billing-sheet ops to finance. Content sprint kickoff (compliance + getting-started first). |
| **Days 31–60** | Hire starts: 2-week support-shadow + product bootcamp, takes Social book. Support shadowing rotation live; tier-1 transfer begins. Learning center v1 live (getting started + compliance + top-10 ticket articles). Managed Marketing menu published in-app and on the site. |

## 7. Risks & the honest list

1. **The hire is the linchpin and the plan overloads them on paper** — creative
   for four product lines + a Social quota. The 50/30/20 split must be enforced;
   if Social growth stalls by month 6, cut the shared-creative load, not Social.
2. **Support transfer fails without content first.** Sequence is content → macros
   → shadowing → transfer. Flipping tickets to support before the learning center
   exists just moves the queue and angers support.
3. **"Free help" habit dies hard** — existing marketing customers are used to
   calling Tony/Jason. The grandfathered/managed distinction needs a clean line:
   *how-to questions → support/learning center; strategy & execution → paid.*
   Expect 60 days of friction; hold the line or the managed line never grows.
4. **Tony is still the bottleneck**: Ads owner + managed lead + sessions +
   external marketing + this transformation. The billing handoff to finance and
   the support transfer are what make this survivable — do those first, not last.
5. **Managed capacity ceiling (~30 accounts)** arrives fast if Tier 1 repricing
   pushes the 40–50 "pays more" accounts toward wanting service for their money.
   Have the second-hire trigger pre-agreed so success doesn't become a queue.
6. **Measure deflection honestly**: if support satisfaction on marketing tickets
   drops, the learning center has gaps — fix content before blaming support.
