Marketing Department · September 2026

## Growth & Retention

**Free trial, built to convert.** One month free of in-app marketing (Reviews,
Broadcasts, Campaigns), opt-out: billing is already on file, so after the free
month the service continues unless the customer cancels.

- **New customers:** trial tied into implementation right out of SMRT Start,
  with pop-ups at 30 / 60 / 90 days since go-live and an automated marketing
  email at 60 days post-go-live.
- **Current customers:** re-engagement trial launched with the new Marketing
  Dashboard — come see your own data, run in-app marketing free for a month,
  decide at day 30. Annual in-app pop-up keeps the offer in front of the base.
- **Baseline evidence:** 125 SMRT Start trials converted at 14–15% as opt-in
  with no structured follow-up; opt-out trials with success-workflow follow-up
  benchmark at 40%+.
- **Future (Q4+):** self-serve opt-in inside SMRT with automated billing for
  in-app services — pop-ups and emails convert without a manual billing step.

**Contracts (retention with a built-in trial).** All packages: 1-year term ·
first month free · 60-day risk-free opt-out. After day 60, mid-term cancellation
requires payment of the remaining contract; otherwise cancel at term end.
(Package names TBD.)

| Package | Services | Discount | Contract /mo | Effective yr-1 discount |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — In-App** | Broadcasts + Campaigns + Reviews | 5% | $1,042.15 | 12.9% |
| **2 — External** | Social + Ads + Website (ad spend not included) | 5% | $853.10 | 12.9% |
| **3 — Everything** | All services, internal + external | 10% | $1,795.50 | 17.5% |

The free month plus discount stack in year one (12.9% / 17.5% effective);
renewals revert to the plain 5% / 10%. Customers get the free month plus a full
paid month inside the 60-day window before commitment locks — a real trial with
real retention.

**Phone: call monitoring + after-hours AI answering (via CTM).**
A SMRT-branded phone product: records and analyzes every store call (missed
calls, price-shoppers, keyword trends, staff handling) and answers after hours
with an AI agent that quotes prices, checks order status, and captures leads.

*Why CTM (research summary):* one platform covers both halves with true white
labeling at a **flat $244/mo with unlimited sub-accounts** — competitors charge
per customer (GoHighLevel: $30–53K/mo at 500 stores) or lack white labeling
entirely. Open API lets us onboard stores programmatically and build our own
SMRT dashboard on top. Building on Twilio ourselves is cheaper per store
($38 vs $62) but costs ~$200K and 4–6 months before the first call — the plan
is CTM now, own dashboard in year one, revisit a Twilio backend at ~300 stores
(migration invisible to customers, margin 69% → 81%). At $199/mo the product
runs **~69% gross margin from day one**, sits at competitor CallRail's top
price while offering more (they have no after-hours answering), and — uniquely —
ties calls to SMRT order data: we can show an owner whether a caller actually
placed an order. **Ask: 10-store pilot, ~$2,200 total, no engineering.**
Launch inbound-only (outbound AI voice = robocall liability), recording/AI
disclosure on every call, SMS kept off the critical path (per-EIN registration).
