Notes captured 2026-09-03 for the next plan draft. Contracting details/options

## Sales growth — in-app marketing promotion mechanics

1. **In-app pop-ups**
   - Current clients: pop-up **once per year** promoting in-app marketing.
   - New clients: pop-ups at **30, 60, and 90 days since go-live**.
2. **Automated email triggers** promoting marketing to customers:
   - New clients: automated marketing email at **60 days since go-live**.
   - Trigger-able message to customers about marketing (reusable automation,
     not one-off sends).
3. **Free trial + contract (the conversion offer)**
   - **First 30 days free, with contract signed up front.**
   - Customer can **opt out any time within the first 30 days, risk-free**.
   - After day 30, the contract takes effect (terms per CFO discussion —
     see `CFO_CONTRACT_DISCUSSION.md`; the free month = 8.33% effective
     discount on a 12-month term, same cost as Season Saver's two 50% months).
4. **Future — Q4 or beyond: self-serve opt-in + automated billing**
   - Customer can opt in to in-app marketing services **by themselves inside
     SMRT**, with **automated billing** for in-app services (no manual billing
     sheet step). This is the end-state that makes the pop-ups/emails convert
     without a human in the loop.
